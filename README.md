# Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Superstore sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, and areas where the business may be experiencing losses.

## Tools Used
- **Excel:** Data inspection, cleaning checks, and exploratory analysis
- **SQL:** Data querying and analysis
- **Tableau:** Interactive dashboard and visual presentation of findings

## Key Findings
- Total sales were approximately **$12.64 million**.
- Total profit was approximately **$1.47 million**.
- Overall profit margin was approximately **11.62%**.
- Technology generated strong sales and profit performance.
- Furniture had the lowest profit margin among the main product categories.
- Tables recorded an overall loss, highlighting an area for further investigation.
- Higher discount levels were associated with weaker profitability in parts of the dataset; this association does not establish causation.
